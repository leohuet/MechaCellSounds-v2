Mécasique was part of different events, including scientific mediation, computer music, but also creation workshops:

  • Souk des Sciences, scientific mediation event, Aix-en-Provence, 2025
  • EAC, art/science creation workshop, in hand with the GMEM, Marseille, 2025
  • Pint of Science, scientific mediation event, Marseille, 2024
  • Journées d'Informatique Musicale, meeting and conference days on computer music creation, Marseille, 2024
  • Science is wonderful, scientific mediation event, Bruxelles, 2024
  • Fête de la science, scientific mediation event, Marseille 2023
  • Nuit Européenne des Chercheur.e.s, scientific mediation event, Marseille, 2023

Project documentation

The Mécasique project was presented at the Journées d'Informatiques Musicales. In this context, an article explaining the project, its progress and the choices made in terms of sound creation, was published. The article is available here.
